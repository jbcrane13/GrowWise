v0.1 — for review Audience: 50+ enthusiast gardeners
Scope: Home, Garden, Plant Detail, Garden Club
Date: April 18, 2026

The current app does too much asking. It asks the user to identify the plant, set up reminders, configure the weather widget, choose a hardiness zone, decide what counts as urgent. Each ask is a chance to drop off.

The redesign reverses that. The app does the work in the background — identification, weather, scheduling, zone-aware tips — and surfaces the result as plain language. The user only has to look, tap, or share. The Garden Club becomes the second pillar: every screen has one obvious way to share what they're growing with people they actually know.

1
Smart enrichment is invisible
Weather, plant ID, hardiness zone, and care schedules just appear. No setup screens, no settings to find. Marked with ✦ where the user might wonder where the info came from.
2
One screen, one job
Each screen answers exactly one question: What needs doing? Where is everything? How is this plant? What's the club up to? Detail is reachable, not in the way.
3
Sharing is built in, not bolted on
Every plant has a "Share to Club" button. The Home screen surfaces club activity. The Club tab opens straight into the feed with a one-tap photo prompt.

What changed.

vs. current build
5 → 4
Tabs collapsed
Reminders folded into Home (where they're already most useful). Journal moves into Plant Detail as a photo strip — it's still there, just stops competing for primary nav.
3 → 1
Setup screens removed
Hardiness zone, weather opt-in, and reminder configuration are now derived from location and plant choice. The user never sees a settings flow on first run — just lands on Home with their first task.
+1
Club is a pillar, not a corner
Club activity surfaces on Home. Every plant has a Share button. The Club tab opens with a "share what's growing" prompt at the top — sharing takes one tap, not a flow.
