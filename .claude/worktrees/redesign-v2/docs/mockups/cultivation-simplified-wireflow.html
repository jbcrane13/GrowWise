Cultivation · 2026-04-20 · v2 pivot

Less tapping.
More growing.

The current app asks too much — set your zone, tag your plant, enable weather, configure reminders. V2 does that work in the background and gives the user three things: a plain-language "what to do today," a garden they can glance at, and a Garden Club where sharing a photo is one tap.

Cream paper · field journal Coral for commits · Moss for care Sage marks smart enrichment
01 · Principles

Three moves that define the rework.

Each principle maps to a concrete decision you'll see in the mockups below.

01

Smart enrichment is invisible.

Plant ID, weather, hardiness zone, scheduling — all derived. No setup screens. Where the app infers something, it shows a small Auto-identified so the user knows why.

02

One screen, one job.

Home answers "what today?". Garden answers "where is it?". Plant Detail answers "how is it?". Club answers "what's the room up to?". Detail is reachable, not in the way.

03

Sharing is built in.

An activity card on Home, a coral Share button on every plant, and a Club tab that opens with a photo prompt — never a cold "create post" screen.
