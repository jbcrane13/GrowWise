docs: Simplified UI v2 — cream paper field journal + 4-tab layout

field-journal aesthetic aimed at the 50+ enthusiast audience. Consolidates
tabs from 5 -> 4, promotes Garden Club to a top-level pillar, and makes all
smart enrichment (plant ID, weather, zone, advice) ambient with a sage
marker rather than a setup step.
