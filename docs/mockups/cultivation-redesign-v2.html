Cultivation Redesign Proposal

Botanical Field Journal

A design direction that moves away from tech-glass-morphism toward something that feels like a curated garden journal crossed with modern editorial design. Warm, grounded, alive.

Color Story

Stone and sage over brown. The dark base is a cool charcoal, not warm mud. Accents are a clean coral and golden amber — warm without being heavy. Sage greens appear naturally in garden context. The overall feel is a stone garden wall at dawn, not a dusty potting shed.

Typography

Instrument Serif for headings evokes botanical illustration plates and garden journals. DM Sans for body text is clean and modern. JetBrains Mono for data gives a precision-instrument feel to numbers and stats.

Heading — Instrument Serif
Your garden is thriving
Body — DM Sans
The basil cuttings you planted on March 12th are showing strong root development. Consider moving them to a larger pot within the next week.
Data — JetBrains Mono
72°F   3 overdue   12 plants   Zone 8b

Key Design Shifts

  • Timeline over task list — Care tasks presented as a timeline with contextual urgency, not a flat checklist
  • Editorial journal — Journal entries styled like magazine articles with hero images, not boring rows
  • Sparkline care history — Visual 30-day care patterns at a glance, not just "last watered" dates
  • Warm floating action — Terracotta FAB with rotation animation, not a flat green button
  • Weather as garden intelligence — Weather strip gives actionable gardening insight, not just temperature
  • Organic bed cards — Garden beds as individually-colored cards with health dot indicators
  • Grain texture — Subtle noise overlay on backgrounds for tactile warmth
  • Serif + mono contrast — Typographic tension between editorial elegance and data precision
