Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Introduction

Cultivation ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Garden Data: Plants, gardens, beds, journal entries, reminders, and photos you create
  • Account Information: Email address (for subscription management), display name
  • Preferences: Hardiness zone, measurement system, skill level

Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Analytics: Feature usage, crash reports (via Apple's frameworks)
  • Device Information: iOS version, device type (for optimization)

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Sync your data across devices via iCloud (CloudKit)
  • Send plant care reminders
  • Process subscription payments
  • Respond to support requests

CloudKit & iCloud Sync

Your garden data is stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This means:

  • Your data is encrypted and stored by Apple, not on our servers
  • You control access to your data through your Apple ID
  • We cannot access your personal garden data
  • Data syncs automatically across your iOS devices signed into the same Apple ID

Garden Club Features

When you join or create a garden club:

  • Your garden becomes visible to other club members (read-only)
  • Your display name and profile photo are shared with club members
  • Club activity (posts, messages) is shared within the club
  • You can leave a club at any time to remove shared access

Third-Party Services

Perenual Plant Database

We use the Perenual API to enrich plant information. Plant searches are sent to Perenual's servers. See perenual.com/privacy.

Apple Services

  • iCloud/CloudKit for data sync (Apple's privacy policy applies)
  • App Store for subscriptions (Apple's privacy policy applies)

Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide our services. When you delete your account:

  • CloudKit data is removed from your iCloud storage
  • Subscription data is retained per legal requirements

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (via the app)
  • Export your data (Settings → Export Garden Data)
  • Delete your data (Settings → Delete All Data)
  • Request a copy of your data

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest (via CloudKit)
  • Secure API key storage in iOS Keychain
  • No third-party analytics or tracking

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
